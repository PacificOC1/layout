# Layout View

Play any level as a bare layout: only gameplay objects, plain colours, a flat background. Switch it from the **LV** button in the pause menu or with **Ctrl+L**.

The same button can also save a **layout copy** of your own levels, main levels and free-to-copy levels into your created levels, or a **low detail copy**: the same level with less to draw, either with low value decoration removed or marked High Detail for GD's own Low Detail Mode.

Physics are never changed.
